# Networked IGO Governance Lab

{% include lab_nav.html %}

## About 

We study how states, institutions, and organizations coordinate under weak or non-binding institutional settings, using tools from network science and computational social science.

### Research Themes
- International cooperation and informal governance
- Network dynamics in IGOs (APEC, ISO, UN bodies)
- Political economy of standards and regulation
- Methods: network analysis, ERGM/TERGM, mixed methods
