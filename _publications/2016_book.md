abstract: >-
  An illustrated introduction to political science. Its eleven chapters cover: the state, ideologies, regime types, parties and interest groups, government structures, public administration, constitutions and courts, elections and participation, political culture, and political change. Each concept is broken down into diagrams for readers with no background in the field, with Taiwanese cases used throughout. I wrote Chapter 4, "Democracy and Dictatorship," covering the definition and preconditions of democracy, varieties of democratic rule, totalitarian and authoritarian regimes, the global trend of democratization, and the process and crises of democratic transition.
