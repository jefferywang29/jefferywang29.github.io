abstract: >-
  This project constructs temporal network data across alliance, IGO, trade, and financial layers over successive historical periods in order to ask under what conditions cooperation networks fragment, substitute, or reconfigure. The questions are concrete and currently unanswered with relational evidence: when a hub state withdraws, does the network reroute around it or lose a component outright? Do middle powers actually fill the gaps left by great-power retrenchment, or do regional institutions absorb the function instead? Which structural signatures precede fragmentation rather than merely accompany it?
